## Remaining risks and open decisions

- **Windows are choices.** The three fixed windows are documented closing-high/closing-low and calendar-year choices, not universal crisis definitions; changing them is a new windows version.
- **Retrospective application.** Today's holdings applied to 2007–2022 windows carry selection and survivorship bias; results are historical and gross of costs.
- **Provider coverage vs inception.** Pre-listing detection depends on Yahoo's first-trade metadata and on its 400 body text; unknown provider limits are reported as coverage gaps, never as inception.
- **Short-window statistics.** Rolling 20-session and COVID-length (23-return) annualized figures are noisy by construction and carry that caveat.
- **Stress data TTL** is the analysis's 1 hour, not the plan's proposed 30-day validated-snapshot TTL; no separate validation step exists.
- Carried forward: no qualified deployed provider or Vercel deployment (DATA-PROVIDERS.md gates unchanged); `/api/stress` is covered by the same gates and returns `UNQUALIFIED_PROVIDER` there.
